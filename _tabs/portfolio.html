Project 01AI Engineering · 2025

Agentic Customer Service

A multi-turn customer-service system for Optik 150 K that combines grounded answers with product, order, payment, and escalation workflows.

Challenge

Customer questions mix product discovery, store policy, pricing, and purchase intent. The system needed to retain context while preventing unsupported answers and unsafe actions.

Approach

I designed a LangGraph ReAct workflow with intent routing, MMR retrieval, structured sales slots, validation, confirmation gates, and human handoff.

System

DeepSeek-V3, Jina Embeddings v3, Qdrant, MySQL, REST APIs, QRIS payment flow, webhooks, and audit logging.

Result

Context Recall increased from 73.6% to 85.6%, while Answer Relevancy reached 92.16% across QA and multi-turn evaluation.

Project 02AI Engineer Intern · 2024

ElAiS

A knowledge assistant built at IOPRI/PPKS to help users explore institutional profiles, oil-palm products, and technical cultivation material.

Challenge

Useful answers were distributed across long technical books, product documents, and institutional sources with different writing styles and levels of detail.

Approach

I iterated from hybrid graph-vector retrieval to a focused Qdrant pipeline, adding query paraphrasing and reranking to improve the quality of final context.

System

LangChain, Qdrant, Jina Embeddings, Jina Rerank, document parsing, Streamlit, and RAGAS-based evaluation.

Result

The project established an evaluation-led retrieval workflow and a maintainable foundation for answering domain-specific questions from internal knowledge.

Project 03Machine Learning · 2026

Book Recommender

An end-to-end recommendation study focused on sparse interactions, duplicate editions, cold start, and realistic serving trade-offs.

Challenge

The dataset looks large, but interaction density is low: median ratings per user and per book are both only one. Multiple ISBNs can also represent the same work.

Approach

I combined item-based collaborative filtering, metadata similarity, and a popularity fallback, then grouped editions to stop the same title appearing repeatedly.

System

Python, pandas, scikit-learn, sparse matrices, offline evaluation, catalog filtering, and a notebook-based reproducible analysis.

Result

The resulting pipeline supports personalized recommendations where interaction history exists and sensible fallback behavior for sparse or new users.

Project 04Data Science · 2026

Customer Segmentation

A customer-analytics workflow that treats cancellations and returns as business signals rather than simply discarding them.

Challenge

Raw retail data contains missing customer IDs, cancellations, negative quantities, and customers whose gross spend hides a high return value.

Approach

I converted transactions into richer customer profiles, including recency, frequency, net monetary value, activity, product variety, and return ratios.

System

Python, pandas, feature validation, log transformation, standardization, K-Means, and cluster-profile analysis.

Result

Four segments were selected for clearer campaign actions: high value, active regular, low activity, and return-heavy customers.
